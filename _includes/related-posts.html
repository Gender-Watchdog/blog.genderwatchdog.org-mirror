{%- comment -%} "More on " block under a post: the 5 newest OTHER posts in the same language that belong to the same entity. An entity with `slug_match` in _data/entities.yml claims every post whose slug contains one of those strings, plus any post that lists it in `entities:` front matter, so old posts need no edits. Renders nothing for the "other" language bucket (mixed languages). A page with `reference_for: ` is listed first, on posts of the same language whose `entities:` front matter includes that id.

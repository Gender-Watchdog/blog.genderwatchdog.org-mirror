On October 10, 2026, the Visual Ping monitor Gender Watchdog set up on Dongguk University's graduate film faculty page fired an alert. The automated summary read: "The faculty page's department designation changed from 영화영상제작학과 to AI영상예술산업학과, reclassifying the listed faculty under the updated department name."[^1] At the top of the roster, 김종완 was still listed as 명예교수 (emeritus professor) with a phone field showing only "-".[^4]

Same faculty. New sign.

---

## What Changed, and When

Three live surfaces on Dongguk's Graduate School of Imaging website now carry the new name:

1. **The faculty page** (the one Visual Ping monitors) — changed between October 3 and October 10, 2026.[^1]
2. **The history page** (`dic.dongguk.edu/page/1042`) — records the formal change as "2027.03 영화영상제작학과 학과명 및 전공명 변경 / 학과명 변경: 영화영상제작학과를 AI영상예술산업학과로 변경 / 전공 변경(신설): 영화영상제작전공을 시네마틱콘텐츠제작전공으로 변경 (AI&비주얼이펙트전공 신설)."[^2]
3. **The homepage navigation** — the breadcrumb dropdown now reads "AI영상예술산업학과."

The timing gap is worth noting: the formal effective date is **March 2027**, but the new name appeared in a school news headline on <a href="https://dic.dongguk.edu/article/awards/detail/214141">August 27, 2026</a> — "AI영상예술산업(영화영상제작)학과 BK21국제학술대회" — and on the faculty page by October 10.[^3] No name-change notice was published on the school's public notice board. No 2027 admissions announcement using the new name has been archived.

The Wayback Machine captures confirm the old name on July 25, 2025[^5] and April 1, 2026[^4] — placing the rename somewhere between April and October 2026, with the new name in operational use at least seven months before its stated effective date.

Dongguk's English website uses the name Graduate School of Digital Image & Contents,[^29] and an April 2, 2026 archive of the site calls this department the Department of Film and Digital Media.[^6]

## The Label Outruns the Faculty

Dongguk's history page announces not just a name change but a new major: **AI&비주얼이펙트전공** (AI & Visual Effects). The roster tells us who would teach it.

The archived faculty page from April 1, 2026 lists five full-time professors (전임교수).[^4] Two are in adjacent fields:

- **김정환** (교수): 시각효과(VFX), 컴퓨터아트 — research areas include Cinematic Interactive Art, VFX, Motion Capture, CG, and Digital Intermediate. His English-page credentials: "Visual SFX, MFA, School of Visual Arts."[^6]
- **김정호** (교수): 애니메이션 — his English-page credentials: "3D Animation, MFA, California Institute of the Arts."[^6]

Both are traditional digital-production specialists. Neither profile mentions artificial intelligence, machine learning, or any AI research. The other three full-time professors are in directing (양윤호), sound design (이원덕), and film editing (이강석). 김종완, the senior professor now listed as 명예교수, specialized in directing and film production.

Across all 18 faculty — full professors, adjunct, visiting, and special appointment — **not one** holds a degree in computer science, machine learning, or artificial intelligence. The only AI reference in the entire public roster is a single research line by 신동헌 (대우교수, the lowest adjunct rank): "the relationship between AI, VR, and editing programs."[^4] That is a tool-user's research question, not AI research. His highest credential is a bachelor's degree in engineering.

The AI&비주얼이펙트전공 rests, on the basis of the visible roster and public record, on one VFX professor and one animation professor — neither with AI credentials — and an adjunct who studies editing software. The public record does not yet show AI capacity behind the new major.

### Who Builds the Tools This Degree Claims to Teach

Generative AI video — the technology the new department name signals — is produced by diffusion models and large language models. Feature-length AI films are arriving in Korean theatrical release: *Steelay* (90 minutes, entirely generated visuals with no live actors or physical sets) opened in October 2026, and *Raphael* (138 minutes, Mateo AI Studio / MBC C&I AI Content Lab) is scheduled to open November 25, 2026.[^23] The 30th Bucheon International Fantastic Film Festival expanded its competition to include feature-length AI works.[^23]

The tools making these films come primarily from Chinese and US developers. Korean studios that want to use them are using commercial tools built by engineers with machine learning doctorates, not film production doctorates. In 2025, director Kang Yun-sung used AI for visual effects on <a href="https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/entertainment/films/20251201/korean-cinema-turns-to-ai-low-budgets-as-industry-slump-continues">"Run to the West"</a> — a car explosion scene that would take four to five days with traditional computer graphics was completed in roughly ten minutes with AI tools, cutting total production costs to approximately ₩1.5 billion.[^24] The film drew only 27,996 admissions against a break-even of at least 200,000 — the Korea Times attributed the shortfall to story and quality, not to the AI tools themselves.[^24]

> *Gender Watchdog analysis:* A graduate student enrolling in this renamed program in 2027 will be taught by faculty none of whom have documented credentials in the technology the program is named for. A student who instead completes a computer science degree — or takes structured online courses in generative AI foundations and prompt engineering from providers in the US, China, or Europe — would acquire a more accurate technical understanding of how the tools they will actually use are built. That understanding is available globally, often at a fraction of the cost of a Korean private graduate degree. Free non-degree routes exist: Busan Film Commission is prioritizing AI film and video training in 2026.[^18] Dongguk's own public record shows neither CS faculty nor any documented AI film workflow curriculum. The credential gap is the problem — specifically because the department has not shown what it is offering instead.

We acknowledge the counter-argument: deep CS knowledge is not strictly required to use generative AI tools, just as cinematographers do not need to understand sensor physics. A production-focused program teaching *how to use* AI tools — workflow, aesthetics, storytelling — is a legitimate offering. But that offering requires a faculty with documented experience of the actual tools and their workflows. The public record shows none.

## Dongguk's Rebrand Cadence

This is not Dongguk's first buzzword pivot. The <a href="https://dic.dongguk.edu/page/1042">history page</a> records the department's own evolution[^2] (the 2027.03 rename entry is confirmed by screenshot; the 2021–2023 entries are from the history page text and have not been separately archived):

- **2021.09:** 콘텐츠비즈니스전공 (Content Business major) added
- **2022.05:** Selected as an "OTT 콘텐츠 특성화 대학원" (OTT Content Specialized Graduate School)
- **2023.03:** 뉴미디어영상제작전공 (New Media Production major) added
- **2027.03:** AI&비주얼이펙트전공 added; department renamed

The pattern: the department's identity stays; the fashionable word on the door changes. The two archived research-visiting professors on the April 2026 roster both listed "OTT영상콘텐츠" as their research area[^4][^7] — a vestige of the last buzzword cycle, now superseded by the next. OTT to AI in four years.

## Korea's AI Label Economy

Korea's government has pledged ₩100 trillion ($72 billion) in AI investments and launched a National Artificial Intelligence Strategy Committee under President Lee Jae-myung.[^8] After round two of the government's domestic foundation model competition (독파모), Vice Minister Ryu Je-myung stated: "미국, 중국에 이은 글로벌 AI 3강의 지위를 더욱 공고히 한 것으로 평가된다" ("This further consolidates South Korea's position as a global AI top-3 power after the US and China").[^9] This came as the evaluation methodology faced growing criticism: inconsistent benchmarking scores were published nine days later,[^10] with broader public criticism continuing into September.[^11]

The environment rewards the AI label. Dongguk's rename fits that reward structure.

**On AI and youth employment:** Bank of Korea data shows that <a href="https://www.ajupress.com/view/20260507160342024">nearly 98% of roughly 211,000 youth jobs lost between July 2022 and July 2025 were in industries with high AI exposure</a>.[^12] A graduate program selling AI film expertise is selling into a labor market where AI is simultaneously the credential and the displacement mechanism.

---

## The Incentives to Carry an AI Label

**Sector squeeze:** The government's budget proposal for 2027 includes ₩328 billion in free-tuition grants for 30 regional national universities, rising to cover all years by 2030.[^13] Korean private university presidents have warned publicly of potential closures.[^14] Dongguk is a Seoul private graduate school, not directly in scope, but the competitive pressure on private institutions is real context.

**AI quota incentives:** The Education Ministry approved new AI departments for 2027 (Konkuk University: 50 places, quota drawn partly from existing computer engineering enrollment).[^15] Whether Dongguk applied for or received an AI-label quota or program grant is not in the public record.

**BK21 timing:** The fifth phase of the BK21 research funding programme begins in September 2027 — six months after this rename's formal effective date. A 2026 pilot funded four AI-convergence research teams, with Chung-Ang University among the recipients.[^16] The department already carries a BK21 research label. Whether the rename was timed to support a BK21 application is inference and cannot be established from the public record.

## Is "AI Film" a Degree?

Dongguk is not alone in claiming this territory, and it is not ahead.

**Chung-Ang University** already operates 첨단영상대학원 with a BK21 "AI-content future industry" research unit and runs the <a href="https://www.dhnews.co.kr/news/view/1065573701805597">Advanced Imaging International Festival (AIIF)</a>.[^17] Chung-Ang is also one of the four teams selected in the 2026 BK21 AI-convergence pilot.[^16]

**Within Dongguk itself:** The undergraduate 영화영상학과 (College of Arts) signed a MOU in May 2026 to train specialists in "AI-based film production and short-form content."[^19] Dongguk now has two separate units — undergraduate and graduate — both claiming AI film, with different names. They are different departments. Do not conflate them.

**Free non-degree routes** exist: <a href="https://v.daum.net/v/8VqtmOwa1A">Busan Film Commission has made AI film and video training a 2026 priority</a>.[^18] The Bucheon International Fantastic Film Festival announced a goal to train 10,000 AI video content creators over five years.[^18]

The field is real, it is developing fast, and the leading producers are not waiting for Korean graduate schools to certify it.

## What a New Name Cannot Reset

The rename changed what the department is called. It did not change who is on the roster.

**김종완** appears at the top of the faculty page — in both the October 3 (old name) and October 10 (new name) captures — listed as 명예교수 with a phone field of "-" and no office address.[^1][^4] The <a href="https://blog.genderwatchdog.org/dongguk-faculty-purge-paper-faculty-eu-cleanup-april-2026/">April 2026 post</a> documented his transition from active 교수 (with office K413 and phone 02-2260-3845) to 명예교수 with every contact line stripped.[^7] That record is now indexed under the new department name.

That is a consequence of how name-keyed records work. Gender Watchdog archived the old name before the rename was finalized; it is now on the permanent record under both names.

## A Hypothesis: Why Now? (GW Analysis — Explicitly Labelled)

The documented facts create a timing cluster. We present them without asserting a causal chain.

**What is documented:**

The department's rename was in operational use by <a href="https://dic.dongguk.edu/article/awards/detail/214141">August 2026</a>, formal date March 2027.[^3] 김종완's transition to 명예교수 with stripped contacts is first documented in the April 1, 2026 Wayback capture.[^4] On November 27, 2025, all five full-time professors of the department donated ₩10,000,000 to Dongguk's 로터스관 construction fund at a ceremony attended by graduate school dean 양윤호, department head 이원덕, and professor 김정호 on the department side, and by university president 윤재웅 on the institution side.[^20][^21]

**The same circle, documented across five years:**

The 2020 COVID donation article names three of the same people who appear at the 2025 ceremony:[^27][^20]

| Person | 2020 role | 2025 role |
|---|---|---|
| 이원덕 | 영상대학원장 (graduate school dean) | 영화영상제작학과장 (dept head) |
| 양윤호 | 영화영상제작학과 교수 (rank-and-file) | 영상대학원장 AND 예술대학장 |
| 김종완 | 영화영상제작학과 학과장 (dept head) | 명예교수, contacts stripped |

By August 2025, 양윤호 also held a third concurrent leadership position outside Dongguk: elected 이사장 (chairman) of 한국영화인협회 (Korea Film Persons Association).[^22]

These are documented administrative transitions, not allegations. What the pattern shows structurally: after a documented institutional failure — the removal from the roster of 차승재, against whom a conviction is on record and whose coercive academic power over students Gender Watchdog documented in a prior post[^28] — the response was removal and internal promotion. The same circle that controlled the department in 2020 controls it in 2025, with positions redistributed among themselves. No independent review is documented.

> *Gender Watchdog analysis:* The rename, the contact-stripping of 김종완, and the collective faculty donation form a timing cluster spanning February to November 2025, with the new name in operational use by August 2026. We note this cluster without asserting a causal link to our advocacy. Gender Watchdog content surfaces on web searches that add "성폭력" (sexual violence) to the department name, and on English-language searches — indicating our materials are findable by researchers and journalists investigating this topic. Plain Korean searches for the department name alone — both "동국 영화영상제작학과" and "동국대 영화영상제작학과" — did not surface GW content on Naver or Daum as of November 2026.[^26] We cannot establish that any of these institutional changes were proximately caused by GW's advocacy. No Dongguk statement acknowledges GW.

**The counter-explanations we cannot rule out:**

- **Mandatory retirement:** Dongguk's university charter Article 50-4 (charter version amended 2018) sets faculty retirement at age 65, effective the last day of the semester in which the birthday falls.[^25] If 김종완 turned 65 in late 2025 or early 2026, the roster change is fully explained without reference to GW. His birth year is not in any public record we have found.
- **AI industry trend:** AI-integrated film is a growing training priority.[^23] The rename fits an international trend, independent of GW.
- **BK21 incentive:** Fifth-phase programme renewal from September 2027 rewards contemporary names; institutional incentive independent of GW.[^16]
- **Rebrand cadence:** This department has changed its name and majors four times in five years. This rename fits the same standing pattern.[^2]
- **Donation precedent:** In June 2020, the same graduate school's faculty donated ₩10M for COVID-19 student scholarships — identical amount, same press-release structure, same academic unit.[^27] The 2025 donation fits a pre-existing pattern of collective giving, not an isolated signal. The 2025 purpose differs (a capital building campaign, not student welfare), but the gesture is structurally the same.

## What This Post Does Not Claim

- It does not claim the rename was intended to conceal anything, was ordered by anyone in particular, or was connected to any individual's conduct.
- It does not claim the collective donation was made in response to GW advocacy. It is a data point in a timing cluster.
- It does not label 이원덕, 양윤호, or 김종완 as perpetrators. Only 차승재 has a documented and convicted case in GW materials.
- It does not claim GW materials surface in plain Korean searches for the old department name. The defensible claim: GW content surfaces when "성폭력" is added, and in English searches.
- It does not claim Dongguk is in financial distress. No Dongguk-specific financial figures have been sourced.
- It does not use KWDI statistics.
